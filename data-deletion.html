Data Deletion

Last updated: 2026-11-10

The "bordemin pipeline" application is a private tool used only by the owner of the bordemin accounts. It keeps a small record of public comments left on the owner's posts (commenter name, comment text, time) so the owner can answer them. It holds no account data about anyone else.

If you commented on a bordemin post

  1. Deleting your comment on the platform removes it from the owner's records at the next daily refresh.
  2. Or ask directly: send a message on Discord with your username and the platform. Your comments are removed from the owner's records within 30 days, and you get a reply when it is done.

If you connected the app to a Facebook or Instagram account

Only the owner's own accounts are connected. To remove the app's access from a Facebook account: Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → select bordemin pipeline → Remove. Any tokens it held stop working at once, and the owner deletes the stored copies.
