Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

This policy covers the "bordemin pipeline" application. It is a private tool, used by one person (the owner of the bordemin accounts), to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts and to answer comments on them.

What it does

  • It signs in to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and X accounts, with the owner's permission, and uploads the owner's own videos to them.
  • It reads the public comments left on the owner's own posts, so the owner can read and answer them in one place.
  • It posts the owner's replies to those comments. Every reply is approved by the owner, one at a time.
  • It reads view counts and similar totals for the owner's own posts and accounts.
  • It reads the basic profile of the signed-in account (display name, handle) to confirm which account it is connected to.

What it stores

  • Sign-in tokens for the owner's own accounts, stored only on the owner's own computers (a laptop and a home server).
  • Comments on the owner's posts that are waiting for an answer: the commenter's public name, the comment text, the time and the post it is on. Answered comments are kept for at most 60 days to avoid replying twice.
  • View counts and similar totals for the owner's own posts.

Comments may be read by an AI assistant the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude) to help sort them and suggest replies. Nothing is used for advertising, tracking or profiling, and nothing is collected about people who do not comment.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Sharing

No data is shared with or sold to anyone. Replies the owner approves are posted publicly on the platform where the comment was left, like any reply.

Deleting data

See Data deletion: anyone can ask for their comments to be removed from the owner's records.

Contact

Questions: reach out on Discord.
