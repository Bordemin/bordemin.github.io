Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-13

This policy covers the "bordemin pipeline" application. It is a private tool, used by one person (the owner of the bordemin accounts), to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

What it does

  • It signs in to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X accounts, with the owner's permission, and uploads the owner's own videos to them.
  • It reads only the basic profile of the signed-in account (display name, handle) to confirm which account it is connected to.

What it collects

  • Sign-in tokens for the owner's own accounts. They are stored only on the owner's computer and are used only to upload videos.
  • Nothing else. No data about other people, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The application is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Sharing

No data is shared with or sold to anyone. The videos uploaded are the owner's own public content.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the application's access at any time in each platform's security settings, and delete the stored tokens from the computer.

Contact

Questions: reach out on Discord.
