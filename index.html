About

I'm borde. I build things in games and film how they hold up when real players attack them. The long videos are guides: one idea per build, shown working (or breaking) in a real match. The shorts are the best moments from those sessions, usually with my squad on voice.

Wardogs is the game while it is in early access. I stream on Twitch, and the community hangs out on Discord.

The posting tool

bordemin pipeline is my own small tool. It posts my own finished short videos to my own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X, at the times I schedule, so each short goes out everywhere after it premieres on YouTube.

  • It is used only by me, on my own accounts. Nobody else signs in to it.
  • It uploads a video and its caption, and reads back whether the post succeeded.
  • It does not read messages, followers or anyone else's data.
